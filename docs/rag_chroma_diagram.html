MarketingAssistant RAG and ChromaDB Map

How editable knowledge cards are seeded into local ChromaDB collections, retrieved by narrow retrievers, and injected into the agents that create audience notes, ideas, posts, and Instagram comment-to-DM automation resources.

2Calls seed_collection() for every collection.
